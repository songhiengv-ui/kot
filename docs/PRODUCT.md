# Product — Kat (កត់, "write it down")

**Promise:** Spend money. Say it. Saved. — All your money, all your accounts, one clear picture.

**Who:** People and small shops in Cambodia juggling ABA, ACLEDA, Wing and cash, in USD and KHR.

**What it is:** a manually-tracked money notebook with Khmer voice/text capture. **Not** a bank, not connected to banks, balances are what the user recorded. Never ask for bank passwords/OTPs.

**MVP success:** on a phone, a user signs up, adds ABA/ACLEDA/Cash with opening balances, records a $5 ABA expense, records an expense by speaking Khmer, fixes the AI result, transfers $100 between accounts (not counted as spending), sees totals, history, simple insights, and switches Personal/Business.

**Tone:** calm, minimal, trustworthy. Business mode says "cash flow", never "profit".
